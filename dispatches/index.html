Where is the blog?

The site's blog lives here inside Blog and Dispatches. It combines story-world evidence drops, political satire essays, reading lists, definitions, and book-club resources.

What topics does the blog cover?

The blog covers political satire, government incompetence, propaganda, public corruption, private power, memory manipulation, dystopian fiction, and political thriller reading guides.

Can these essays be read without the novel?

Yes. Every public dispatch is written to stand alone while also opening a route into the novel's characters and themes.
