Subject File: Dijon Garnier

Operational posture: Private capital behaving like jurisdiction. French-born, the richest man alive, terrible public speaker, and fluent in turning national exhaustion into procurement contracts.

1. Public Mask

Garnier does not sell domination. He sells relief from disorder. Exhausted citizens accept a tool promising less pain; exhausted institutions accept a private partner promising fewer questions; exhausted politicians accept funding that arrives with language pre-written.

2. Five-Step Private Logic

  1. Enter as assistance.
  2. Become indispensable.
  3. Make departure prohibitively expensive.
  4. Convert necessity into compliance.
  5. Rewrite the record as prudence.

3. Technology Layer: Tecca, BankIt, NeuroClick

Tecca supplies technological prestige. BankIt converts everyday friction into visibility. NeuroClick enters under a therapeutic promise of mercy while giving power a sleek interface over memory itself. Agreement is fragile because people change their minds; curated forgetting removes the material needed to disagree.

4. Perception Doctrine

“Dijon knew war was shaped by perception.” “Any good marketer knows you have to convince the consumer before they buy the product.” Garnier does not need the throne. He owns the room around it.
