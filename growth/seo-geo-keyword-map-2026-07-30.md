## Page architecture

- `/dispatches` is the public Blog and Dispatches hub.
- Definition content leads with a self-contained answer inside the first 200 words.
- Reading-list content uses comparison tables and direct recommendations by mood.
- Resource content uses numbered prompts and a one-hour discussion format.
- Every new article includes named authorship, Article schema, FAQPage schema, canonical URL, Open Graph metadata, descriptive imagery, visible FAQ content, external sources, and internal links.
- Existing pages receive route-specific visible answer blocks and matching FAQPage schema.
