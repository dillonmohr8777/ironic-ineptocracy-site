## Ninety-day execution

### Days 1–14: establish discoverability and measurement

- Keep all 26 canonical pages valid, indexable, internally linked, and present in the sitemap.
- Submit changed URLs through IndexNow after every material publishing batch.
- Monitor Search Console indexing, query impressions, and page-level clicks every day.
- Track lifetime users, daily users, seven-day users, sessions, page views, engaged sessions, and top landing pages.
- Confirm that every new article enters both the Blog / Dispatches hub and at least three contextually relevant internal-link paths.

### Days 15–45: build topical clusters

Publish two strong pages per week, prioritizing:

1. Ineptocracy vs. kakistocracy
2. Modern political satire novels
3. Books about government incompetence
4. Funny dystopian books like *1984*
5. Political corruption novels about billionaire influence
6. Political thriller book-club questions printable guide
7. How political satire works
8. Bureaucracy satire books
9. Propaganda in dystopian fiction
10. Memory manipulation in political fiction
11. Political satire vs. parody
12. Dark-comedy political books

Each page must answer one primary intent, include original analysis, cite authoritative sources, use descriptive real imagery, add FAQ and Article schema when appropriate, and link readers into the book rather than ending as a detached SEO article.

### Days 30–75: earn referral authority

- Build a vetted weekly list of 10 relevant book reviewers, political-fiction bloggers, reading-list editors, librarians, podcasters, newsletter writers, and book-club organizers.
- Pitch the useful resource that matches each recipient: the definition guide, reading list, comparison page, or book-club questions.
- Offer original commentary, interviews, excerpts, or a tailored discussion guide instead of requesting a naked backlink.
- Track every target, pitch, response, placement, referring URL, and resulting GA4 referral traffic.
- Do not buy bulk links, place irrelevant guest posts, or use automated comment spam.

External outreach remains draft-only until the exact recipient list and message are approved.

### Days 45–90: compound the winners

- Refresh pages earning impressions but sitting below position 10.
- Expand pages with impressions and weak click-through rates using better titles, direct answers, and richer comparison sections.
- Merge or redirect pages that compete for the same intent.
- Turn every successful article into five distribution assets: one short video, one carousel, one quote graphic, one social thread, and one email section.
- Add supporting internal links from character and world pages when a topic naturally connects to the story.
