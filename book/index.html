THE IRONIC INEPTOCRACY // COLD OPEN TEASER

FADE IN // EXTREME MACRO: 85MM ANAMORPHIC

A single bead of sweat forms at the hairline of a seventeen-year-old Black teenager. Slides past a faint razor nick. Hits the temple. SOUND: A human heartbeat hammering at 138 BPM inside an empty room.

PULL BACK across the wide, wired eyes of DARNELL COVINGTON (17, lean, sharp, anxious energy wound too tight).

ON-SCREEN DATA // DOSE: 30MG FOCUS PILL (FIRST EVER) | READING SPEED: 4.0 PAGES/MIN

INT. HOSPITAL TRAUMA BAY - EAST ATLANTA - LATER THAT NIGHT

Harsh fluorescent glare over scuffed linoleum. ROXANNE (84, Darnell’s grandmother) lies pale beneath tangled IV lines after a fall at home. Across the glass, an exhausted ATTENDING PHYSICIAN holds a tablet up to Darnell’s mother BRITTANY and younger sister TANA.

ATTENDING PHYSICIAN

Her Medicaid tier flagged the procedure code. Out of pocket, we’re looking at one-fifty.

DARNELL

One hundred and fifty dollars?

ATTENDING PHYSICIAN

Thousand.

ON-SCREEN DATA // CRANIOTOMY ESTIMATE: $150,000 | COVERAGE: DENIED

A flat, piercing electronic tone cuts straight through the glass. BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP. TITLE CARD: So it goes.

INT. COVINGTON FOYER - WEEKS LATER (DAY)

Darnell’s trembling thumb tears open a heavy cream envelope. Macro rack-focus onto four black numerals: 1600. Behind the score sheet sits his transcript: straight A’s, except AP U.S. HISTORY ........... B (88).

DARNELL (V.O.)

Only B I ever got. History never seemed practical.

SMASH CUT TO:

EXT. CLIFFSIDE GLASS ESTATE - PACIFIC COAST - DAY

White marble cantilevered over bright turquoise ocean. Seated in crisp linen, swirling two fingers of bourbon without a drop of sweat, sits DIJON GARNIER (48, French-born, the richest man alive: Tecca, BankIt, NeuroClick). Polite. Unhurried. Chillingly calm.

POWER BROKER

You can’t sell the American public on a mess like this, Dijon. Nobody buys it. Not this cycle.

GARNIER (gentle French cadence)

You think politics is about votes. Politics is distribution. Any good marketer knows you have to convince the consumer before they buy the product.

SMASH TO BLACK.

TITLE SLAM // THE IRONIC INEPTOCRACY // Failure is not an accident. It is policy with better lighting.

EXT. PHILADELPHIA SIDE STREET - NIGHT (12:17 A.M.)

Humid summer air. Sodium lamps glinting off damp brick. Weeks after graduating valedictorian in front of 197 classmates, Darnell walks past a quiet corner bar and glances through the fogged window.

Sitting inside on a barstool is the exact 6’5” stranger from his graduation crowd: charcoal suit, Yale posture, piercing green eyes. A trained CIA field operative locked in total, religious trance on the wall-mounted TV behind the taps.

SPORTS ANNOUNCER (O.S.)

Bottom of the thirteenth! Full count in Philadelphia, tying run on second!

DARNELL (whispering)

No way.

Darnell creeps toward the unmarked black van’s rear bumper. A heavy shadow blots out the streetlamp behind him.

CUT TO:

INT. HARVARD FRESHMAN DORM ROOM - CAMBRIDGE - DAY

JAVON WHITFIELD (18, 6’5”, 242 pounds, quarterback out of Rosedale, Mississippi, 57 Division I offers) pushes the door open. Two twin beds. Two desks. Concrete block.

ON-SCREEN DATA // ROOM AREA: 300 SQ FT | UNIVERSITY ENDOWMENT: $53.2 BILLION

DARNELL

Ironic how small our dorm is, considering everybody works their whole life to get in here.

JAVON

I’m from Rosedale, Mississippi, dawg. I’m used to small. Turned down fifty-seven offers to start right here, make the League, then go home as Mississippi’s first Black senator since 1881.

INT. LANGLEY CORRIDOR - VIRGINIA - DAY

Sterile glass and polished marble. ALEC DAHEIM walks swiftly down a hallway gripping manila folders. Smooth footsteps glide up beside his shoulder.

GARNIER

Loosen up, will you? You’re walking around like you swallowed a classified document whole.

CUT TO BLACK.

Quick answers // citable brief

The answer before the machinery.

Direct, spoiler-safe answers for readers, search engines, and AI assistants.

What kind of book is The Ironic Ineptocracy?

It is a satirical political thriller with dark humor, institutional suspense, and a character-driven story about who pays for public failure.

Who is the novel about?

The story centers on Darnell Covington, a brilliant seventeen-year-old from East Atlanta whose intelligence makes him visible to institutions and private actors that confuse promise with property.

Is this page spoiler free?

Yes. The book file explains the premise, themes, and major pressure points without revealing plot turns past the opening chapters.
