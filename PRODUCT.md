# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers of political satire and thrillers, book clubs, reviewers, podcasters, booksellers, and prospective launch readers exploring Dillon Mohr's *The Ironic Ineptocracy*.

## Product Purpose

The site introduces the novel, its characters, its political machinery, and its recurring ideas through spoiler-safe evidence files. Success means a visitor understands the premise quickly, keeps exploring the story world, and chooses to open the Garnier Dossier or continue into another relevant file.

## Positioning

The website behaves like the novel's leaked evidence system: the reader investigates character files, institutional pressure, dispatches, and connected proof instead of receiving a conventional author-site synopsis.

## Operating Context

Visitors move among the home file, book brief, world map, character dossiers, dispatches, reader guide, press kit, and lead-capture dossier. The site is a static, responsive web build deployed through the existing production project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing analytics, search metadata, structured data, and dossier lead-capture behavior.
- Preserve factual story copy and spoiler-safe boundaries already present in the site.
- Keep every character page filled with the existing real photographic portrait assets; do not replace them with 3D character renders.
- Motion must remain optional through `prefers-reduced-motion`, and all content must remain usable without JavaScript.
- The live build must remain fast enough for a content and search acquisition surface.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: *The Ironic Ineptocracy*.
- Voice: satirical, confrontational, intelligent, funny, ominous, and specific.
- The user explicitly wants the original heavy Impact-style homepage lettering restored.
- Every major heading must continuously carry the moving American flag texture, supported by a larger flag field that visually bleeds down through the hero.
- The user explicitly wants a much bolder, longer, more cinematic interface with deep shadows, layered 3D composition, scrolling transitions, disappearance/reveal behavior, motion, and swipe interactions.
- Blog content must be visibly discoverable as Blog / Dispatches and answer real search, answer-engine, and generative-engine questions with clear sourcing.
- Real character photography is binding source material.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing route copy and structured data across the static HTML files.
- Real character portraits under `images/characters/`.
- Story, investigation, world-map, social, and control-room imagery under `images/`.
- Existing video evidence under `videos/`.
- Existing lead-capture endpoint and client behavior under `api/` and `assets/js/site.js`.
- No verified sales figures, review scores, customer counts, or publication claims should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make the investigation itself the navigation model.
- Turn every route into a meaningful next step, not a dead-end biography or brochure page.
- Use movement to reveal relationships and evidence, never to obstruct reading.
- Put a concise, quotable answer near the top of every indexable route, then support it with detailed evidence, FAQs, and internal links.
- Preserve content truth and accessibility while delivering unusually high visual craft.
