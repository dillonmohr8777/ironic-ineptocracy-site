Avigail

A hotel front-desk clerk with piercing green eyes who is thoroughly unimpressed by urgency, flattery, or badges.

Front desk intelligence. Zero patience for scripted authority. “There are no rooms for the next six hours. Come back later.”

Who is Avigail?

Avigail enters through a front desk, which matters because the novel rarely treats safety as a grand event. Sometimes safety is a room key, a sharp read of posture, and a clerk deciding that the frightened person in front of her still counts as human.

Her intelligence is practical rather than ornamental. She clocks hesitation, fear, and scripted lies within seconds.

Role in the novel

Avigail operates in the thresholds between official spaces, bringing dry humor and steady instincts into rooms that powerful people expect to bend automatically.

“I have no answer and absolutely no idea. Maybe that’s the fun of it.”
