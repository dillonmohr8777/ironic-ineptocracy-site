CASE 017 // FEATURE SCRIPT TEASER (COLD OPEN)

FADE IN // EXTREME MACRO: 85MM ANAMORPHIC

A single bead of sweat forms at the hairline of a seventeen-year-old Black teenager. Slides past a faint razor nick. Hits the temple. SOUND: A human heartbeat hammering at 138 BPM inside an empty room.

PULL BACK across the wide, wired eyes of DARNELL COVINGTON (17, lean, sharp, anxious energy wound too tight).

ON-SCREEN DATA // DOSE: 30MG FOCUS PILL (FIRST EVER) | READING SPEED: 4.0 PAGES/MIN

INT. HOSPITAL TRAUMA BAY - EAST ATLANTA - LATER THAT NIGHT

Harsh fluorescent glare over scuffed linoleum. ROXANNE (84, Darnell’s grandmother) lies pale beneath tangled IV lines after a fall at home. Across the glass, an exhausted ATTENDING PHYSICIAN holds a tablet up to Darnell’s mother BRITTANY and younger sister TANA.

ATTENDING PHYSICIAN

Her Medicaid tier flagged the procedure code. Out of pocket, we’re looking at one-fifty.

DARNELL

One hundred and fifty dollars?

ATTENDING PHYSICIAN

Thousand.

ON-SCREEN DATA // CRANIOTOMY ESTIMATE: $150,000 | COVERAGE: DENIED

A flat, piercing electronic tone cuts straight through the glass. BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP. TITLE CARD (quiet white serif, center frame): So it goes.

INT. COVINGTON FOYER - WEEKS LATER (DAY)

Darnell’s trembling thumb tears open a heavy cream envelope. Macro rack-focus onto four black numerals: 1600. Tilt down to his transcript behind the score sheet: straight A’s down the page, except one stubborn stain near the bottom: AP U.S. HISTORY ........... B (88).

DARNELL (V.O.)

Only B I ever got. History never seemed practical.

SMASH CUT TO:

EXT. CLIFFSIDE GLASS ESTATE - PACIFIC COAST - DAY

White marble cantilevered over bright turquoise ocean. An infinity pool hangs over the drop like a dare.

Seated in crisp linen, swirling two fingers of bourbon without a drop of sweat, sits DIJON GARNIER (48, French-born, the richest man alive: Tecca, BankIt, NeuroClick). Polite. Unhurried. Chillingly calm.

Across the table sits a nervous Washington power broker nursing a glass of water.

POWER BROKER

You can’t sell the American public on a mess like this, Dijon. Nobody buys it. Not this cycle.

Garnier stands. Walks to the glass rail overlooking sailboats hundreds of feet below. Smiles faintly.

GARNIER (gentle French cadence)

You think politics is about votes. Politics is distribution. Any good marketer knows you have to convince the consumer before they buy the product.

SMASH TO BLACK.

TITLE SLAM // THE IRONIC INEPTOCRACY // Failure is not an accident. It is policy with better lighting.

EXT. PHILADELPHIA SIDE STREET - NIGHT (12:17 A.M.)

Humid summer air. Sodium lamps glinting off damp brick. Weeks after graduating valedictorian in front of 197 classmates, Darnell walks slowly past a quiet corner bar. He spent forty-seven minutes watching a black SUV hang behind him on the highway.

Parked illegally by the curb sits a matte-black unmarked van. Darnell glances through the bar’s fogged front window and stops.

Sitting inside on a barstool is the exact 6’5” stranger from his graduation crowd: charcoal suit, Yale posture, piercing green eyes. A trained CIA field operative. He is not scanning the exits. His green eyes are locked in total, religious trance on the wall-mounted TV behind the taps.

SPORTS ANNOUNCER (O.S.)

Bottom of the thirteenth! Full count in Philadelphia, tying run on second!

The man leans forward over his whiskey glass like national security hangs on a slider.

DARNELL (whispering)

No way.

Darnell creeps toward the black van’s rear bumper to read the unmarked government plate. Pulls up his phone. A heavy shadow blots out the streetlamp behind him.

CUT TO:

INT. HARVARD FRESHMAN DORM ROOM - CAMBRIDGE - DAY

JAVON WHITFIELD (18, 6’5”, 242 pounds, quarterback out of Rosedale, Mississippi, 57 Division I offers) pushes the door open. They stand shoulder to shoulder in the threshold because two steps puts you against the far wall. Two twin beds. Two desks. Concrete block.

ON-SCREEN DATA // ROOM AREA: 300 SQ FT | UNIVERSITY ENDOWMENT: $53.2 BILLION

DARNELL

Ironic how small our dorm is, considering everybody works their whole life to get in here.

JAVON (laughs loud, drops onto a squeaking mattress)

I’m from Rosedale, Mississippi, dawg. I’m used to small. Turned down fifty-seven offers to start right here, make the League, then go home as Mississippi’s first Black senator since 1881.

INT. LANGLEY CORRIDOR - VIRGINIA - DAY

Sterile glass and polished marble. ALEC DAHEIM walks swiftly down a hallway gripping manila folders, jaw clenched tight. Smooth footsteps glide up beside his shoulder.

GARNIER

Loosen up, will you? You’re walking around like you swallowed a classified document whole.

Hold on Alec’s piercing green eyes reflecting in the glass.

CUT TO BLACK.

What is The Ironic Ineptocracy?

The Ironic Ineptocracy is a satirical political thriller by Dillon Mohr about brilliance, friendship, donor power, public failure, propaganda, and manipulated memory.

What does ineptocracy mean?

Ineptocracy means government or leadership by incompetent people. The novel turns that idea into a thriller about what happens when visible public failure creates room for disciplined private power.
